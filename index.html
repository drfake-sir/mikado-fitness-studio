Questions 問

Questions,
answered.

Everything you need before your first visit. Still unsure? Send a message — a coach will reply.

mikadofitness8@gmail.com

No questions match that search. Try “trial”, “timing” or email us.

01Is the trial really free?

Yes. Your first session is complimentary — walk in, meet a coach and try a session before you commit. Confirm a slot by email or Instagram so someone is expecting you.

02I’ve never trained before. Will I keep up?

That’s exactly what the beginner batch is for. You’ll start with the basics, learn the movements at your pace and get guidance on what to do next. Every class is scalable.

03What are the studio timings?

The studio runs morning and evening batches, seven days a week. The timetable above is a sample — confirm the current batch times with the studio before you plan your visit.

04Do you offer personal training?

Yes. One-to-one coaching is available for focused goals, form work and accountability. Ask about current PT slots and pricing when you enquire.

05What should I bring for my first session?

Comfortable training clothes, supportive shoes and a water bottle. Bring a towel if you like. Arrive a few minutes early so a coach can show you around.

06How do memberships and payment work?

Monthly, quarterly and annual memberships are available. The prices on this page are sample placeholders, and no payment is taken online. Confirm the current plan sheet, payment options and terms directly with the studio.
